Popular Destinations

A few remarkable places to include in your Lesotho itinerary.

Nature

Maletsunyane Waterfall

Visit the impressive waterfall near Semonkong and enjoy the surrounding highland landscapes.

Heritage

Thaba Bosiu

Explore the historic mountain stronghold connected to King Moshoeshoe I and the Basotho nation.

Nature Reserve

Tšehlanyane Nature Reserve

Enjoy forest trails, mountain scenery, and the peaceful surroundings of this highland reserve.

Mountains

Maloti Mountains

Take in Lesotho's dramatic mountain views and find opportunities for outdoor exploration.

Adventure

Afriski and Oxbow

Explore the highlands around Oxbow and discover seasonal mountain activities.

Culture

Basotho Villages

Learn about local traditions, everyday life, and the warm hospitality of Basotho communities.

Things to Do

Make your trip memorable with highland experiences.

Pony trekking

Explore rugged routes on a Basotho pony.

Hiking

Walk mountain trails and take in the scenery.

Cultural visits

Discover Basotho heritage and local traditions.

Photography

Capture waterfalls, valleys, and mountain views.
