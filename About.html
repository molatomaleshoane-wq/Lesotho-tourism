About Lesotho

Discover the people, heritage, and highland landscapes of the Kingdom in the Sky.

The Kingdom in the Sky

Lesotho is a mountainous, landlocked country surrounded by South Africa. Its dramatic highlands, rivers, and valleys are closely connected to the history and everyday life of the Basotho people.

The Basotho nation was founded under King Moshoeshoe I. Sesotho and English are the country's official languages. Lesotho is known for its distinctive cultural traditions, mountain scenery, and warm hospitality.

Basic information about Lesotho
Topic Information
Capital Maseru
Official languages Sesotho and English
People Basotho
Currency Lesotho loti (LSL); South African rand is also used
Districts Berea, Butha-Buthe, Leribe, Mafeteng, Maseru, Mohale's Hoek, Mokhotlong, Qacha's Nek, Quthing, and Thaba-Tseka

Why Visit Lesotho?

  • See dramatic mountain scenery and highland valleys.
  • Experience Basotho history and cultural heritage.
  • Enjoy activities such as hiking and pony trekking.
  • Visit waterfalls, nature reserves, and historic sites.
  • Meet welcoming communities and learn about local traditions.
