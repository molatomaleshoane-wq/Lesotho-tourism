Explore Lesotho

Discover mountain landscapes, rich heritage, and unforgettable adventures.

View Destinations

Popular Destinations

Explore some of the Kingdom of Lesotho’s best-known places.

Maletsunyane Waterfall

Visit the spectacular waterfall near Semonkong and enjoy hiking and scenic viewpoints.

Thaba Bosiu

Learn about Basotho history at the mountain stronghold associated with King Moshoeshoe I.

Tšehlanyane Nature Reserve

Explore highland trails, native plants, and peaceful mountain scenery in this protected reserve.

Afriski Mountain Resort

Enjoy seasonal snow activities and mountain views in the Maluti Mountains.

Sani Pass

Take in dramatic highland scenery along this famous mountain route. Check local travel conditions before your visit.

Sehlabathebe National Park

Discover remote highland scenery, rock formations, and wildlife in Lesotho’s first national park.

Where to Stay

Choose accommodation that suits your trip and budget.

Comfort

Mountain Lodges

Relax in a lodge and enjoy highland views and local hospitality.

Outdoors

Camping

Stay close to nature at designated campsites. Check facilities and weather before travelling.

Cultural experience

Guesthouses and Homestays

Experience local hospitality and learn more about Basotho culture.

Things to Do

Lesotho offers outdoor adventures and cultural experiences throughout the year.

  • Pony trekking with a local guide
  • Hiking and mountain sightseeing
  • Visiting heritage sites and villages
  • Seasonal snow activities
Plan or Book a Visit

Mountain weather can change quickly. Pack warm clothing, plan your route, and check local conditions before travelling.

Ask permission before photographing people, respect local customs, and leave natural areas clean.
